# Approach Note — AIE-CASE: Color-Invariant Saree Design Recognition
We use a pretrained ResNet18 as a visual encoder and add a 256-D embedding head trained with supervised contrastive loss. Strong color/appearance augmentation (ColorJitter, grayscale, blur) encourages embeddings to focus on surface motifs rather than palette. Images are resized/cropped to 224×224 and normalized. Training uses P×K sampling (4×4) for positive/negative pairs. The 59 labeled images were split by design into train/validation/test (12/2/4 designs) to avoid design leakage. Retrieval uses cosine similarity; verification uses ROC-AUC/EER and TAR@FAR.
